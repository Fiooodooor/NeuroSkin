PVDF dynamic response — simulated signal

PVDF characteristics

PVDF estimates use an assumed local film-strain transfer, linear d31/d33 constants, feedback capacitance and RC charge-leak response. PVDF cannot continuously sense static pressure with a simple charge amplifier. This is not piezoelectric FEM or hardware validation.
